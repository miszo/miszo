I'm a Staff Software Engineer and technical leader at Housecall Pro, working on frontend platform and architecture. I co-own our frontend platform strategy and own our micro-frontend approach: breaking a large monolith into independently deployable modules without slowing product teams down.

My focus is making the right path the easy path: clear architecture, solid tooling and production-readiness standards that teams can adopt on their own.

## What I care about

- **Architecture and developer experience.** Platform work is product work, and the users are other engineers.
- **Tooling.** I'm a terminal and Neovim person. Working in Neovim taught me how LSP and linters actually work and how they plug into editors. My setup lives in [dotfiles](https://github.com/miszo/dotfiles).
- **Neurodiversity.** I'm autistic (diagnosed in 2023) and I care about building inclusive engineering teams. I favor explicit documentation, clear written decisions and architecture that doesn't depend on tribal knowledge.

Off the clock I'm a proud father, a lucky husband and a long-suffering Arsenal fan.
